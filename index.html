Common Questions

Quick Answers

Can I start the process today?

Yes. Once you contact us, a consultant will reach out within 1 hour to get things moving. The clock starts the same day you submit your details.

Does 5 days include weekends?

No - the 5-day timeline is based on working days, Sunday to Thursday in the UAE. Weekends and public holidays are not counted.

What if my documents are not ready?

We can still start the consultation and trade name reservation while you gather documents. Your consultant will tell you exactly what is needed and what can wait.

Can I really get a license in 5 days?

Yes - for freezone setups, 5 working days is achievable when all your documents are in order. Our team manages the entire process to keep it on track.

Do I need to be in Dubai to set up?

No. Many freezone setups can be completed fully remotely. We will guide you through every step from wherever you are.

What is included in the setup fee?

Trade license, government registration, establishment card, and PRO services. Visa and office costs are quoted separately and outlined clearly upfront.
